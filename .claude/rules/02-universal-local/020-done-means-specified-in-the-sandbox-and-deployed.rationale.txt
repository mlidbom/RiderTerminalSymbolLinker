# Why done means specified in the sandbox and deployed

It replaced the rule "Build the plugin and report its location when done", which made Magnus's installing a zip, with
Install Plugin from Disk and a Rider restart, the last step of every fix: until 2026-11-17 that was the only way the
plugin was seen working. The sandbox Rider and its specifications check it without him, and his Rider reloads a
deployed build without a restart.

Magnus, 2026-11-17, on the old rule: "That rule is probably obsolete. From what you are saying it sounds like you can
just deploy the new version to me and rider will not even need to restart?" He then set `-Didea.auto.reload.plugins=true`
in his Rider's VM options and said yes to this rule.

Untested when the rule was written: that his everyday Rider reloads a deployed build when he switches to its window. In
the sandbox, the reload was triggered by asking Rider to look at its plugins folder, through the plugin's observation
channel, which his Rider doesn't run. If deploys don't reload there, look at whether switching windows makes Rider
look at the plugins folder, before changing the rule.
